## 1. SOL Staking

Direct delegation to Dawn Labs' validator with multiple staking options.

### Native Staking
Direct delegation with **0% commission** — the simplest way to earn staking rewards on Solana.

### LST Staking (dawnSOL)
Liquid staking through **dawnSOL** on Sanctum. Stake SOL and receive dawnSOL, a liquid staking token that can be used across DeFi while earning staking rewards.

Exclusive kickback plans for large-scale stakers. [Contact us](https://dawnlabs.tech/#contact) for details.
